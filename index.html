About the project

Smart Meal Guide pulls the weekly menu of the Antalya Bilim Üniversitesi – Personel Yemekhanesi automatically from the university website (menu PDF). Every dish is matched with our dish database and shown with its photo, ingredients, dietary labels, nutrition facts per portion, allergen warnings and an estimated recipe. New dishes that are not in the database yet are flagged for completion.

Nutrition data

Nutrition values per portion are calculated from the estimated recipe using TürKomp (Turkish Food Composition Database) as the reference. Values are estimates and may differ slightly from the served portion.
